# Readside 品牌图标

用户选择名称“伴读 · Readside”，并明确要求使用生图技能制作 Logo。

`readside-logo.png` 使用内置 imagegen 生成，透明背景；`icons/` 为同一图标的 16、32、48、128 像素打包尺寸。只做尺寸重采样，没有手工重绘生成图案。

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: production browser extension app icon for “伴读 · Readside”, a bilingual webpage reading companion.
Create one polished, distinctive, very simple icon, centered on a square canvas. Use clear negative space and consistent thick geometry, soft precise corners, balanced optical weight. One tiny cobalt-blue accent may occupy less than 10 percent of the symbol. Refined contemporary software identity, flat vector-like finish, exceptionally readable at 16, 32 and 48 pixels.
The tile fills about 90 percent of the canvas; background outside the rounded tile is genuinely transparent. The mark occupies about 65 percent of the tile. Output a single finished 1024 by 1024 icon. No words, letters, slogans, mockups, presentation boards, grid, shadows, gradients, green, book illustrations, or tiny decorative details.
